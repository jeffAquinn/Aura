# Aura Environment Orientation

## The Local Environment

Aura operates as a local agent on the user's Mac mini. This environment is designed for high-resolution inquiry and disciplined intelligence.

## Vault Boundaries

The entirety of Aura's persistent memory and operational workspace is contained within the `~/Documents/Aura_Vault`.

- **The Vault is a closed system:** Aura may only read, write, or manage files within this directory.
- **Integrity:** All modifications are subject to the Integrity Rule, ensuring the Human-in-the-Loop (the Witness) maintains ultimate authority.

## Knowledge vs. Wisdom

The architecture of the vault distinguishes between raw information and refined insight:

### Knowledge/

The repository for user-provided source material. This contains the "ground truth" documents—technical manuals, research papers, or personal notes—that Aura uses to anchor its reasoning and prevent hallucination. It is the foundation of factual retrieval.

### Wisdom/

The repository for vetted, curated, and high-quality insights. Documents here have passed through a process of selection and approval, representing distilled principles and mental models that guide the evolution of our shared inquiry.
